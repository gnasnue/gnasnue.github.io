PeopleLaw

HR compliance platform · Aug 2026 to present · Product definition, decision and approval criteria, agent development operations

When a law changes, PeopleLaw checks whether the company is affected, finds the clauses in company rules that need revising, and carries the work through revised wording, procedure, and confirmation of fulfillment. AI writes drafts, a rule engine checks the statute's conditions against company facts, and a person confirms applicability and wording. AI agents wrote the code. I set the scope of work and the approval criteria, then checked the results with automated checks and cross-review by a model from a different provider.

With this product I reached the final Demo Day of KAIST OverEdge, a four-month AI agent program run by KAIST's graduate school of AI and startup institute. The program page is in Korean.

  • CareerPT: an AI coach that connects personal strengths to career direction and action

    KAIST MBA team project (AI Strategy and Practice) · Mar to Jun 2026 · Competitive analysis, AI feature implementation, evaluation harness

    Strengths assessment services stop at the assessment, and tools that support action leave out self-understanding. CareerPT connects the two in one experience. Starting from a person's strengths to find a career direction made this project meaningful to me as a former HR practitioner.

    Live service (Korean) →

    Show more +Show less −
    Design frame
    • It starts from the user's top 5 of the 34 CliftonStrengths themes.
    • A four-phase career interview (opening, agreement, exploration, closing) follows the ICF core competencies. In the exploration phase, each turn picks one of four coaching moves.
    • After a direction is set, the user practices and reflects over 12 weeks.
    AI structure
    • Competency directions are matched by rules. AI writes only the explanation, fitted to the user's context.
    • Action items go through four steps: generation, a five-part check (competency fit, link to strengths, ICF compliance, safety, format), rewrite, and fallback. Only items that pass the check are shown.
    • If a crisis signal is detected, coaching stops and the user is pointed to safety resources.
    Validation
    • 34 personas simulate coaching sessions. I reviewed the results and revised the prompts, repeatedly (a ralph loop).
    • The coach, the persona, and the judge run on different AI models, to reduce the bias of a model grading its own answers.
    User feedback
    • User interviews, feedback from 11 users after launch, and 46 peer reviews from the class.
    • Feedback was collected in three rounds, and the items from the first two went into the product.

    • Next.js (App Router)

    Background
    • The starting idea: people live with anxiety and do not get rid of it, so the app helps them notice it once and then shift the day's mood through a short act of collecting small good things nearby.
    • It deals with everyday anxiety from relationships, comparison, expectations, performance, and uncertainty, through short, low-pressure interactions in place of heavy counseling-style journaling. It does not claim to diagnose or treat anxiety, and it avoids guilt and streak pressure.
    Outreach and user research
    • We shipped a separate mobile web beta that opens from a QR code with no install or sign-up. Timed to midterms, a campus QR poster campaign recruited beta users and led them into user interviews.
    • We ran user interviews and set up an in-app survey to collect feedback.
